01 · METHOD

Same four moves, every time.

No frameworks, no lengthy discovery calls. A repeatable pipeline from "no website" to "signed client."

01

Recon

Sweep a commercial hub — Wuse 2, Lekki, Ikorodu — for businesses with strong reviews and zero web presence. The gap usually shows up in the reviews themselves.

02

Extract

Pull the real logo and lock the brand's actual hex values. Every demo is color-matched to the business, never a generic template palette.

03

Build

Ship a single-file HTML/CSS/JS site — fast, dependency-free, easy to hand off or host anywhere. Real copy, real location, working details like live local time where it fits.

04

Pitch

Send the finished site, not a proposal. The business is looking at their own name and colors on a working page — the conversation starts from "yes."
